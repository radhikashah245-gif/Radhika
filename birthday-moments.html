For You, On Your Birthday

01

Your smile

It has this quiet magic that can soften any heavy moment and make everything feel lighter.

02

Your heart

Kind, warm, and sincere in ways that are rare and unforgettable. It leaves a mark long after the moment is gone.

03

Your dreams

Even the ones you keep close to your heart deserve to bloom, and I hope this year brings you closer to each one.

Your peace

May life give you gentleness when you need it, confidence when you doubt yourself, and calm when you feel overwhelmed.

May your birthday be full of laughter, warm wishes, beautiful surprises, and the kind of happiness that stays with you long after the candles are out.
